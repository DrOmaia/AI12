1. Get the three tools ready

The course teaches principles that work in any assistant. You need access to at least one of these three, and it is useful to try two so you can compare answers.

  • Gemini: sign in with a Google account.
  • ChatGPT: sign in with an email, Google or Apple account.
  • Claude: sign in with an email or Google account.

Free accounts are enough for most of the course. Some features used on Days 8 to 11 (projects, custom assistants, skills, agent modes, file uploads) differ by tool and plan. Where a feature is missing, the lesson gives a fallback, usually pasting the text as standing instructions at the start of a chat.

Before you start
  • Use a personal or university account you are allowed to use for this purpose. Check your university's policy on which AI tools are approved.
  • Open each tool once and send a harmless test message, such as "Explain what a learning outcome is in two sentences."
  • Find where each tool keeps its settings, help pages and the "new chat" button. You will use them every day.

Feature names, limits and prices change often. When this course and a tool disagree, trust your tool's help pages.

2. Privacy and data settings to check

Do this once for each tool, then again whenever you change plan or account. The names of the settings differ, so look for the ideas below and check your tool's help pages for the exact wording.

Saudi context

Student records, grades, national ID numbers and similar personal data should not go into a public AI tool. Saudi personal-data rules (PDPL) and your university's own policies apply, so check your university's policy and ask your data-protection or IT contact if unsure. This page is general guidance, not legal advice.

Red line for this course

Your teaching assistant never handles student personal data, never assigns grades and never sends anything on its own. Use your own course materials and anonymised or invented examples.

3. Back up your progress

AI12 saves your progress, notes and profile in this browser on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere. That is good for privacy, but it means clearing your browser data, using a private window, or switching device will lose your progress. Download a backup file from time to time, and keep it somewhere you control.

The backup contains your name and notes. Treat the file as personal.

To move to another device: download the backup here, open AI12 on the other device, then restore the file on this page. Restoring replaces the progress currently stored in that browser.

4. Reset the course

This deletes your profile, answers, notes, review cards and progress from this browser. It cannot be undone, so download a backup first if you might want anything back.

5. Troubleshooting

  • My progress disappeared. You may be in a private window, using another browser, or have cleared site data. Restore from your backup file.
  • The Copy button does nothing. Select the text by hand and copy it. Some browsers block copying on pages opened from a local file.
  • The page looks wrong or old. Refresh the page with Ctrl+F5 (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac).
  • My name is wrong on the certificate. Click the icon at the top right, edit your profile, save, and reload the certificate page.
  • A tool says a feature is unavailable. It may depend on your plan or country. Use the fallback in the lesson and check your tool's help pages.
